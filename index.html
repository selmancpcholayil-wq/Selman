Before you begin

A few useful
answers.

We confirm the project details before final delivery.

What does the ₹1,999 offer cover?

A simple one-page business website with a responsive design and the information agreed for that page. Extra pages, ecommerce, booking, payment integrations, and ongoing maintenance need a separate quote.

Is my domain included?

No. The domain name and renewal are paid separately. Your site can be reviewed before you buy or connect a domain.

Will the SEO questions guarantee a Google ranking?

No. The questions help build clear page titles, descriptions, local business details, headings, and useful content. Search ranking depends on many factors and cannot be guaranteed.

Can customers change their information later?

Your project brief is saved to your account so you can sign in and update it. Generated previews can be regenerated from the revised details.

Are testimonials created automatically?

No. The generator only uses testimonials you provide and are authorized to publish. It does not invent reviews or customer claims.
