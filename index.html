What does the ₹1,999 offer cover?

It covers a simple one-page business website with a responsive layout and the information agreed for that page. We’ll confirm the final scope with you before work starts.

Is the domain included?

No. Domain registration and renewal are extra and paid separately. The price depends on the domain name and provider you choose.

Are hosting, business email, or extra features included?

They are not included in this starting offer unless we confirm otherwise in writing. E-commerce, booking, payment integration, additional pages, and ongoing maintenance need a separate quote.

What should I prepare?

Your business name, a short description, services, contact details, and any logo or photos you want to use. If something is missing, we can agree on the best next step together.
